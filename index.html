Indian Contract Act, 1872Section 10
§

All agreements are contracts if they are made by the free consent of parties competent to contract, for a lawful consideration and with a lawful object, and are not hereby expressly declared to be void.

  • Lawful offer and acceptanceSec. 2
  • Competent partiesSec. 11
  • Free consentSec. 13 to 14
  • Lawful considerationSec. 2(d), 23
  • Lawful objectSec. 23
  • Not expressly declared voidSec. 24 to 30
Valid contractSec. 2(h)
